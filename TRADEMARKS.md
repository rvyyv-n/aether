# Branding policy

The code of Aether is free to use, change and share under the GPL-3.0 (see [LICENSE](LICENSE) and [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md)). The name "Aether", its logo and its artwork are not part of that grant.

## Forks and modified versions

You may:

- Fork, change and redistribute the code, including commercially, under the GPL.
- Say that your project is "based on Aether" or "a fork of Aether", with a link to https://github.com/rvyyv-n/aether.
- Keep the required attribution and licence notices (see [NOTICE.md](NOTICE.md)).

You may not:

- Use the name "Aether", or a name easily confused with it, for your fork.
- Use the Aether icon, logo or other brand artwork. Use your own.
- Suggest that your version is the official Aether or is approved by the project.

## Official builds

Only the project's own releases are official Aether builds. The official installer, the code-signing certificate and the update channel belong to the project. Forks must not reuse them or point their users at them.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/rvyyv-n/aether/issues.
